1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the Windows app, its optional AI setup assistant (when a build includes it), and the public download website handle information.

Today’s public Windows beta (dated October 24, 2026) is a single-camera viewer. It does not include Ara. The description of Ara, the Cloudflare Worker, and Google below applies when a later build turns the assistant on, and to our planned production design.

If we change hosts, billing tiers, or how video is routed, we will update this policy so it matches what is actually live.

2. Factual assumptions (read this first)

These points are taken from the app source on the private GitHub branch phase-1-build (the October 24, 2026 beta), from /workspace/reolink-viewer/project-plan.md, and from /workspace/reolink-viewer/ara-backend/ including worker.js. The main branch of LeaveUmWet/Reolink holds docs; the application source lives on phase-1-build. Anything marked assumption should be re-checked against the exact installer you ship.

  1. Camera credentials are stored on the user’s Windows PC. In the phase-1-build source, passwords are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key wrapped by Windows DPAPI, and are not returned to the page in plaintext. Logs are intended to mask secrets. This is not Windows Credential Manager.
  2. Local user / admin passwords for the app are hashed (for example bcrypt) and stored on the PC.
  3. Live video does not always stay on your network. If you connect by local IP / RTSP, the stream is pulled and viewed on your PC (a local process may proxy or transcode it on localhost). If you connect by Reolink UID and leave server assistance / cloud relay on, video and login traffic may pass through Reolink’s servers. In the October 24, 2026 build, that UID relay option has been on by default. This policy does not claim that video always stays local.
  4. Snapshots, time-lapse frames, and optional recordings (when those features exist) are saved to folders you choose. They are not uploaded by our app to Contemporary Innovations, LLC, except that a UID/cloud path may already send live video through Reolink as described above.
  5. Diagnostic logs are written locally (for example under %APPDATA%/Reolink/logs/) with rotation. Raw passwords are supposed to be masked.
  6. Ara (planned; not in the October 24, 2026 beta). Retrieval stays in the app. The app sends the question text and retrieved setup-document chunks, plus a session id and a stable install id, to a Cloudflare Worker we operate. The Worker holds the Google API key as a secret (it is not in the Windows installer). The Worker forwards the question, chunks, and a grounding system prompt to Google’s Gemini API, using the hosted model gemma-4-26b-a4b-it (Gemma 4). Camera credentials, tokens, API keys, and RTSP URLs that embed usernames and passwords are not supposed to be sent. The Worker rejects obvious secret patterns.
  7. Voice (when enabled): speech-to-text and text-to-speech run on the user’s PC. Only the transcribed text (plus retrieved chunks) is sent to the Worker. Microphone audio is not sent to Cloudflare or Google in the current design. Push-to-talk is the intended mode, not an always-on microphone.
  8. A license-key check is planned (not necessarily in the October 24, 2026 beta): the app may send a license key and a hashed machine fingerprint to a licensing server, and may cache a signed token locally.
  9. Auto-update checks of GitHub Releases, if enabled, contact GitHub.

If a shipping build differs, update this policy before you ship it.

3. Information the app stores on your computer

The following typically stay on the PC where the app is installed, unless you export or copy them, or unless a connection path (such as Reolink UID relay) sends data to a vendor as described above:

  • Camera, NVR, and account usernames and passwords, and related connection settings, in encrypted local storage.
  • Layout and preference data.
  • Local user accounts for the app (password hashes).
  • A local install id used when Ara is enabled (for rate limits and usage totals, not as your name).
  • License token or trial state, if licensing is enabled.
  • Logs of app events.
  • Optional snapshots, time-lapse images, and recordings, in the folders you choose.
  • If Ara’s retrieval index is built on the PC, a local copy of setup-document text used for search.

If you share or email a profile file, you may also share the credentials inside it. Treat exported profiles as sensitive.

4. Camera streams, footage, and audio

How live video and audio travel depends on how you connect:

  • Local IP or RTSP: the stream is viewed on your computer and is not uploaded to Contemporary Innovations, LLC.
  • Reolink UID with server assistance / cloud relay enabled: video and login traffic may be routed through Reolink’s servers. Reolink’s own terms and privacy policy then also apply to that traffic. You can use a local IP/RTSP connection instead when the camera is reachable on your network.

If you turn on local recording, scheduled or event-triggered recording, snapshots, time-lapse, talk-back (two-way audio), or on-screen overlays, those features run on your computer and, where a cloud relay is on, may also involve the vendor path above. You are responsible for complying with recording-consent, workplace-notice, and surveillance laws that apply to your site (see the Terms of Use). The app does not obtain consent from people who appear in or are heard on your cameras.

5. What Ara transmits (when a build includes Ara)

When you use Ara:

  • **Sent to our Cloudflare Worker, and then to Google (Gemini API, model gemma-4-26b-a4b-it):** the text of your question, the retrieved setup-document chunks, a session id, an install id, and the system prompt the Worker adds so Ara stays on the setup docs. Google returns the answer text to the Worker, which returns it to the app.
  • Not sent, under the project rule: camera passwords, tokens, API keys, and RTSP URLs that contain credentials. If you type a secret into the chat, the app should strip or refuse it and the Worker should reject obvious secret patterns. Change the password if you think you exposed it.
  • Voice: your microphone audio is converted to text on your PC; only that text is sent. Ara’s reply may be read aloud on your PC.
  • Install id: generated on your PC and sent so the Worker can apply per-install rate limits and count usage. It is not meant to be your name or email.

Google’s use of Ara prompts depends on which Gemini API tier is live. This policy must be kept in sync with the actual project:

  • Unpaid / free tier: Google may use prompts (including system instructions) and generated responses to improve Google products, and human reviewers may read them. Do not type sensitive, confidential, or personal information into Ara on this tier. Source: Gemini API Additional Terms, effective 23 March 2026.
  • Paid Services (API access through a Cloud project with an active billing account): Google does not use prompts or responses to improve its products. Google may still log them for a limited time for abuse and safety. Same terms.
  • Contemporary Innovations, LLC plans to link billing and run Ara under Paid Service terms before real customers use the assistant. A private internal test may still run on the free tier until billing is attached. The About screen and this policy will state which tier is live. If you are an end customer, do not assume paid terms apply until we say they do.

We do not use Ara questions to train our own public foundation model. We do not sell your questions.

If the Worker or the Gemini API is unreachable, the app should fall back to static help and the setup wizard and should not keep retrying in a way that leaks questions to an unexpected endpoint.

A developer-only path that calls Google directly from a machine with a local API key may exist for our own testing. That path is not supposed to ship in customer installers.

8. Retention

  • On your PC: profiles, footage, and logs remain until you delete them, uninstall the app, or a log-rotation rule removes old log files. Uninstall may not delete recordings you saved under Documents or another folder you chose.
  • Ara Worker usage logs (from worker.js as of November 24, 2026): the Worker stores request counts and token totals, keyed by calendar day (America/New_York) and by install_id. It does not store the question text, the retrieved chunks, or the answer. Per-install daily counters expire after about 48 hours. Project-wide daily totals expire after about 40 days.
  • Google: retention of prompts and responses is governed by Google’s Gemini API terms and the live tier (see section 5). On the unpaid tier, content may be used to improve Google products. On Paid Services, Google says it may log prompts and responses for a limited time for abuse and safety.
  • Website and license logs: keep only as long as needed for security, abuse prevention, license enforcement, and legal obligations, then delete or anonymize. A numeric period should be filled in here before publication: [website log retention period].

10. Your choices

  • You can use the viewer without Ara; the add-camera wizard is intended to work with the assistant off. Ara is not in the October 24, 2026 beta.
  • You can choose a local IP / RTSP connection instead of a Reolink UID relay when your network allows it, so live video is not sent through Reolink’s servers.
  • When Ara is available, you can choose typing instead of voice, and you can decline microphone access.
  • You can delete local profiles, logs, and saved media from your PC.
  • You can stop using the download site at any time.
  • If a CCPA or similar request right later applies to you, contact us at the email above. At first-customer scale we may not be a “business” under the CCPA; we will still try to help with access and deletion requests for information we actually hold (for example a license record or an install id in Worker logs). We cannot delete copies Google has already processed under its own terms.

11. Security

We design the app so camera secrets stay on your computer and are encrypted at rest, so the Google API key is held in the Worker rather than the installer, and so Ara is not given passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for the physical and network security of the PC, the cameras, and any account you use with a camera vendor.

12. International users

We are based in Florida, United States. If you use the download site, or Ara, from another country, information may be processed in the United States by us, by Cloudflare, by Google, by GitHub, and (if you use a UID relay) by Reolink.
